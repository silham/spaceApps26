| D16 | **Predict the coming seasons from a seasonal forecast plus history; real calendar.** After a farm loads, the app shows the coming months: ECMWF SEAS5 seasonal-forecast odds (Open-Meteo Seasonal API, 51 members, 36 km) of below / near / above normal rain and temperature for two 3-month windows, today's simulated soil water, and daily bands of rain, Tmin, Tmax and soil water. Comparisons of user-built rotations (D15) run on the same prediction. **The forecast never becomes weather:** the water balance runs only on real NASA POWER days; the forecast weights which past years are drawn for the first agricultural-year block (tercile-conditioned resampling of historical years, as in FResampler1's conditional block sampling, Han & Ines 2017, *Climate Risk Management* 18:51–65, doi:10.1016/j.crm.2017.09.003; multiplying rain and temperature over two windows and the concentration cap are engine extensions). Forecast members are placed against the model's own hindcast climatology (Open-Meteo's monthly ensemble-mean anomaly: model normal = ensemble mean − anomaly): rain as a share of normal (the model's normal at the US test farm was 1.2–1.4× POWER's, so a millimetre offset would overstate wet odds), temperature as an additive anomaly. POWER is compared the same way against its 1981–2016 mean (the SEAS5 hindcast period, Johnson et al. 2019, doi:10.5194/gmd-12-1087-2019). **Real calendar:** the record runs to the latest POWER day (no longer to 2025-12-31); the horizon starts the day after the last day with usable rain and temperature and runs 24 months; a background refresh fetches newer weather (> 3 days old) and a newer forecast (> 7 days old) when a farm opens. **Today's soil water** is simulated from one observed year under what the farmer says is on the field (new optional FarmerContext fields), bare fallow otherwise, stated beside the number; it replaces the 0.5-of-field-capacity start and the per-path warm-up for user farms. ks-field-2 keeps its fixed 2027–2028 model calendar. | Resolved (M1, with the user) | 2026-11-15 | M1 (engine M4, data M3, UI M2 to review) | User asked to predict rain, temperature and soil water and to suggest rotations under that prediction. Weighting past years, instead of feeding raw SEAS5 members to the water balance, was chosen so sources are never mixed (guide §6) and SEAS5's unassessed level bias against POWER cannot reach irrigation or yield numbers. **Method assumptions (engine choices, not calibrated):** rain and temperature terciles and the two windows are independent; member spread used as delivered; weights blended with equal weights just enough that ≥ 10 effective past years carry them (`MIN_EFFECTIVE_YEARS`); 3-month windows, at most 2; one-year spin-up from 0.5 of field capacity, carried state spread evenly over the profile; no irrigation before today. Forecast skill is not evaluated here (no hindcast verification at the site). Open-Meteo's free API is for non-commercial use (CC BY 4.0 data, attribution shown beside every forecast view); a commercial deployment needs their licence. Next (planned, not built yet): crop nutrition traits and soil-health rule indicators; suggested rotations (non-dominated set ordered by the farmer's priorities). |

**Content**

- **Overview content is the frame's illustrative content.** Strategy names, crops, metric values, sampling metadata, run ID `R-014`, field area and constraint values come from `frontend/src/demo/figmaDemo.ts`, shown under the frame's own DEMO labels. **Unspecified states and interactions**

- **Views without a frame** (Rotation planner, Data Journey, Evidence & validation) reuse the dashboard's card, control and status-chip patterns and show API data only.
- **Outdated state.** The frame shows results outdated because the irrigation limit changed. Any further constraint edit is appended to the banner. "Run comparison" calls `POST /runs`; while that returns 501 an inline alert is shown under the banner and the results stay marked outdated. Choosing a strategy and exporting stay disabled while outdated.
- **Constraint dropdowns offer only their current value** until the supported-edit set is decided (D6).
- **Disabled with a reason (tooltip):** Vegetation and Data footprints map layers, "Irrigation needed" mode, "Replay a dry year", "Edit farm details".
- **Map is the static illustrative image** with the frame's overlay (cut order: map tiles → static image). MapLibre is not used yet.
- **Replay.** The frame's scrubber is a static image; it is a draggable range input here. Timeline columns are equal width, so the playhead moves piecewise through an illustrative calendar; both column boundaries (21 Apr, 4 Aug) are placeholders chosen so that 18 Jun lands where the frame draws its playhead. - **Sidebar is sticky** at viewport height; in the frame it runs the full page height.
- **Heat-exposure row** (frame node `28:458`) was not fetched from Figma because the MCP call limit was reached; it is rebuilt from the frame screenshot and layer outline, using the info icon in place of the frame's thermometer icon. Re-fetch and replace when the limit resets.
- **Range bars** on the strategy cards use fixed display axes (water stress 0–35 days, soil cover 170–270 days) so the three strategies line up; the frame's bar positions imply the same axes. The axes are illustrative until real results exist.
- **Mode toggle** (Fixed allocation / Irrigation needed) is the frame's segmented control; "Irrigation needed" is disabled with a reason because the run has no such results.
- **Layout is fluid above 1240 px** and matches the frame at 1440 px.
